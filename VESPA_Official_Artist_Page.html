About VESPA

VESPA is an emerging Nigerian artist and performer whose artistry blends global pop, rap, R&B and hip-hop. Her music explores love, identity, ambition, vulnerability, self-discovery and the experiences that shape a young artist finding her voice.

Her performance style combines expressive vocals, confident rap delivery, storytelling and stage presence. VESPA aims to create music that people can feel, remember and connect with while developing a career across music, acting and modelling.
